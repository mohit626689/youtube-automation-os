# ABC Zoo TV — High-Retention Kids Scripting Master Template

This template enforces the exact structural, auditory, and psychological rules required to achieve **> 75% Average Percentage Viewed (APV)** in the competitive preschool/toddler category on YouTube.

---

## The 5 Rules of Toddler Retention Engineering

1. **The 3-Second Sensory Hook:** Zero fluff, no channel intros, no logos. The video must open immediately with a visual surprise (character popping up), a sharp audio chime (`*pop*` or `*boing*`), and a direct greeting.
2. **The "Bouncing Ball" Rule:** Words appearing on screen must have a visual focal indicator (a bouncing star, glowing circle, or animated character head) synchronized to the syllables.
3. **The 4-Second Visual Refresh:** Toddlers lose focus if a scene is static. Camera angles, object scales, or background color tones must subtly transition every 3 to 5 seconds.
4. **Call-and-Response Participation:** Pause for 1.5 seconds after asking a question (*"Where is the red apple? Can you point to it?"*) followed by positive reinforcement (*"You found it! Good job!"*).
5. **The Next-Episode Character Handoff:** In the final 10 seconds, the next mascot character must appear on-screen to pass the torch, keeping autoplay and suggested clicks flowing seamlessly.

## Master Script Template (Markdown Standard)

```markdown
# Episode Title: [Letter/Concept] Song: [Character Name] & [Fun Activity]
* **Target Runtime:** 2:15 – 2:45
* **Lead Character:** [Name & Animal Mascot]
* **Key Vocabulary / Phonics Sound:** [e.g. /a/ /a/ Apple, Airplane, Accordion]
* **BPM / Rhythm:** [e.g., 112 BPM, Cheerful Ukulele & Marimba]
* **Visual Style:** 3D Clay-Pixar Stylized, Soft Lighting, Primary Color Dominance

---

### [0:00 - 0:08] SCENE 1: THE SENSORY HOOK & GREETING
* **Visual:** Camera zooms rapidly into [Bright Environment]. [Character Mascot] pops up from behind [Colorful Focal Prop] waving excitedly.
* **Audio / SFX:** Playful ascending xylophone slide + soft spring *boing*!
* **Spoken Intro:**
  "[CHARACTER]: Hi friends! I'm [Character Name]! Look what I have today... It's the Letter [X]!
  Can you say [X] with me? /[sound]/ /[sound]/ [Object]!"

---

### [0:08 - 0:38] SCENE 2: VERSE 1 — THE PHONICS RHYME
* **Music:** Bouncy acoustic ukulele kicks in with steady drum clap rhythm (112 BPM).
* **Visual:** [Character] hops joyfully across oversized colored stepping stones. Giant [Object 1] drops down with a soft jelly bounce.
* **Song Lyrics (AABB Rhyme Scheme):**
  "[Letter] is for [Character], having fun today,
  [Letter] is for [Object 1], hip hip hooray!
  /[sound]/ /[sound]/ [Object 1], shiny and bright,
  Singing Letter [X] brings so much delight!"
* **Visual Transition (0:22):** Camera pans left, floating bubbles reveal [Object 2].

---

### [0:38 - 1:12] SCENE 3: THE HIGH-ENERGY CHORUS (PHYSICAL MOVEMENT)
* **Music:** Marimba and brass swells, tempo feels lively and danceable.
* **Visual:** On-screen bouncing ball highlights uppercase and lowercase letters "[X] [x]". [Character] performs a signature silly wiggle dance.
* **Song Lyrics:**
  "[Letter], [Letter], [Letter]! Let's jump up high!
  Reach for the [Object 1] in the sunny sky!
  Wiggle your ears, clap your hands with cheer,
  The happy Letter [Letter] is finally here!"
* **Interactive Cue:** Visual prompt flashes: "CAN YOU JUMP?" with cheerful giggle SFX.

---

### [1:12 - 1:45] SCENE 4: INTERACTIVE "SPOT THE OBJECT" GAME
* **Music:** Music drops to a soft rhythmic marimba tick-tock groove.
* **Visual:** Three colorful floating gift boxes or bubble clouds appear.
  - Box 1: [Target Object starting with Letter X]
  - Box 2: [Distractor Object 1]
  - Box 3: [Distractor Object 2]
* **Spoken Interaction:**
  "[CHARACTER]: Let's play a game! Which one starts with /[sound]/?
  Is it the [Distractor 1], the [Target Object], or the [Distractor 2]?
  Point to the /[sound]/ [Target Object]!"
  *(1.5-second pause with playful thinking music)*
  *SFX: Sparkle ding!*
  "[CHARACTER]: That's right! You found the [Target Object]! High five!"

---

### [1:45 - 2:10] SCENE 5: VERSE 2 & GRAND FINALE CHORUS
* **Music:** Full ensemble returns with joyous clapping and celebration rhythm.
* **Visual:** Confetti, gentle floating stars, all previously found objects float around [Character].
* **Song Lyrics:**
  "[Letter] is for [Object 3], flying in the air,
  Singing with your friends everywhere!
  [Letter] [Letter] [Letter], now we know the sound,
  The happiest letter that we ever found!"

---

### [2:10 - 2:30] SCENE 6: NEXT-EPISODE SEAMLESS HANDOFF
* **Music:** Soft outro melody fade.
* **Visual:** [Next Mascot Character, e.g. Barnaby Bear] peeks from behind a tree holding Letter [Next Letter].
* **Spoken Outro:**
  "[CHARACTER]: Great job learning Letter [Letter] today!
  Look who's coming next! It's my best friend [Next Character]!
  Click right here to sing with [Next Character]!"
* **On-Screen Elements:** 
  - Left Card: Video link to Next Letter Episode.
  - Right Card: Link to "Full Alphabet Mega-Compilation Playlist".
  - Center: Channel Mascot Subscribe Avatar.
```
