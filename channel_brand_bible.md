# ABC Zoo TV — Channel Brand Bible & Creative Visual Standard

Welcome to the definitive creative and technical style guide for **ABC Zoo TV** (`@ABCZooTv`). This document governs visual design, character consistency, color science, audio design, and voice direction across all 100+ automated episodes.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

* **Channel Name:** ABC Zoo TV
* **Tagline:** *"Where Letters Come to Life with Animal Friends!"*
* **Target Audience:** Toddlers and preschoolers aged 1 to 5 years, early learners, parents, and kindergarten teachers.
* **Core Mission:** Deliver joyful, sensory-rich alphabet phonics and positive habit-building songs that capture attention, build early literacy, and give parents complete confidence in screen time.
* **Content Rating:** Family Safe / YouTube "Made for Kids" (strict COPPA compliance).

---

## 2. Visual Style & Aesthetic Standard

To ensure high click-through rates and deep viewer loyalty, **every visual asset must strictly adhere to the unified "ABC Zoo 3D Clay-Pixar" aesthetic**. No realistic animals, no low-poly assets, and no generic clip-art.

### Master Character Prompt Prefix
```text
3D Pixar and claymation style, adorable stylized cute baby animal, huge expressive sparkling glass eyes, friendly warm smile, soft rounded geometry, smooth tactile silicone clay texture, bright pastel studio lighting, rim lighting, 8k resolution, cinematic depth of field, vibrant colorful background --ar 16:9 --v 6.1
```

### Color Palette Architecture
Children aged 1–5 respond most enthusiastically to high-saturation, high-contrast primary and secondary colors:
* **Primary Sky Blue:** `#29B6F6` (Trust, calm, open space)
* **Sunny Yellow:** `#FFD54F` (Energy, optimism, visual anchor for focus)
* **Vibrant Grass Green:** `#66BB6A` (Nature, growth, Allie Alligator's signature)
* **Warm Coral Red:** `#FF5252` (Apples, attention grabbers, hearts)
* **Playful Tangerine Orange:** `#FFA726` (Enthusiasm, warmth)
* **Sweet Berry Purple:** `#AB47BC` (Curiosity, imagination)

### Typography Rules
* **Logo & Thumbnail Font:** Bold, extra-thick rounded sans-serif (e.g., *Baloo 2*, *Fredoka One*, or *Quicksand Bold*).
* **Text Styling:** High-contrast white lettering with a thick 8px dark blue/black stroke (`#1A237E`) and a soft drop shadow.
* **Max Words on Thumbnails:** Maximum 2 to 3 words (e.g., `"LETTER A!"`, `"BRUSH TEETH!"`, `"COLOR RED!"`).

## 4. Sonic & Audio Identity

* **Tempo / BPM:** 105 – 120 BPM (energetic enough to dance, slow enough for toddlers to articulate phonics sounds).
* **Core Instrumentation:**
  * Acoustic Ukulele (warm, friendly foundation)
  * Marimba & Glockenspiel (sparkly, melodic preschool ear-candy)
  * Bouncy Bassline (rhythmic head-nodding groove)
  * Soft handclaps and shaker percussion (invites physical clapping)
* **Vocal Style:**
  * Cheerful, expressive child or female soprano narrator (warm kindergarten teacher tone).
  * Hyper-clear articulation on vowel sounds and phonics consonants (/a/ /a/ Apple).
* **Audio FX Palette:**
  * Bouncing *boing* / cartoon spring sounds when letters pop on screen.
  * Musical xylophone ascending slide when a question is solved.
  * Gentle children's cheer (*"Yay!"*) on interactive breakthroughs.

```
│ [TOP LEFT]             [TOP CENTER]          [TOP RIGHT]  │

│                                                           │
│                        [CENTER]                           │
│                   Main Mascot Character                   │
│                (Big Eyes, Active Motion)                  │
│                                                           │
│                                                           │
│ [BOTTOM LEFT]                               [BOTTOM RIGHT]│
│ Current Phonics Object                       Bouncing Ball│
│ (e.g. Shiny Red Apple)                       Lyrics Box   │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Safe Margins:** Keep all critical character faces and text at least 10% away from outer edges to prevent clipping on mobile devices and tablet cases.
2. **Shorts Safe Zone:** Ensure 9:16 vertical exports place characters and titles within the center 60% vertical band to avoid YouTube Shorts UI overlays (title, like button, comments).
