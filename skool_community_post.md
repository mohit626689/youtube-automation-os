### 🧠 How It Works (The 5 Universal Engines):

#### 1. 🛠️ Interactive Onboarding Wizard (`setup.py`)
When you clone the repo and run `python3 setup.py`, it asks:
- Your channel name & handle
- Your niche or **an inspiration/competitor channel URL you want to model**
- Your preferred visual aesthetic (3D Pixar-Clay, Dark Cinematic 8K, Greco-Roman Statues, Retro VHS Glitch, Cyberpunk Neon)
- Your voiceover style (from 400+ Microsoft Edge neural voices)

It automatically generates your **`pipeline_config.json`**, **`channel_brand_bible.md`**, and a tailored **100-video roadmap** in under 60 seconds!

#### 2. 🎙️ 400+ Authentic Neural Voices (Microsoft Edge TTS)
Zero robotic pitch-shifting artifacts. Whether you need a deep cinematic documentary narrator (`ChristopherNeural`), a philosophical British speaker (`RyanNeural`), an eerie horror narrator (`EricNeural`), or a sweet preschool voice (`AnaNeural`), it articulates words with natural emotional pacing.

#### 3. 🎬 Dynamic Zero-Overlap Video Compositor
We solved the biggest glitch in automated video rendering: **voice and subtitle collisions**.
- **Dynamic Sequential Chaining:** Uses `ffprobe` to measure the exact millisecond length of every spoken sentence, inserting natural breathing pauses.
- **Zero-Overlap Subtitles:** Advanced SubStation Alpha (`.ass`) typography with animated bounce-ins and strict clearance buffers so text never stacks on screen.
- **Procedural Audio FX:** Sound effects (whooshes, pops, chimes, impacts) lock dynamically to spoken words.
- **10 Rotational Subtitle Design Themes:** Dynamic font rotating (`Chalkboard SE`, `Arial Rounded MT Bold`, `Marker Felt`, `Comic Sans MS`, `Arial Black`).

#### 4. 📊 vidIQ Score 96/100 SEO & Automatic Playlists
- **High-CTR Question Hooks:** Dynamic titles that score 96/100 on vidIQ (e.g. *"Can You Find the Bubbles? | Letter B Phonics Lesson for Kids | ABC Zoo TV"* or *"How X Built a Secret \$10B Empire | Magnate Vault"*).
- **3-Paragraph SEO Descriptions:** Optimized for search discovery, timestamps, and full scripts.
- **Automatic Playlists:** Automatically routes 16:9 Landscape videos into learning/documentary playlists and 9:16 vertical videos into Shorts playlists.

#### 5. ⏰ 100-Day Autonomous Cloud Scheduling (8:00 AM & 5:00 PM Daily)
Using YouTube Data API v3's native `publishAt` cloud scheduling:
- **Slot 1 (08:00 AM):** Core Full HD Landscape Episode
- **Slot 2 (05:00 PM):** High-Energy Vertical YouTube Short
Once scheduled, **YouTube's cloud automatically turns the video Public at that exact minute**, even if your computer is completely turned off or offline!
